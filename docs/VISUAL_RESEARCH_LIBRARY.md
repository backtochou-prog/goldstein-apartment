# Visual Research Library — Goldstein apartment

Status: first pass, 2026-11-16. Built **without** a copy of the film.
Everything here comes from public, lawful sources: official studio pages, studio press stills,
the 2015 shooting script, and interviews with the production designer, set decorator and VFX vendor.
Nothing was ripped, decrypted or taken from a pirate source.

Images are cached privately in `reference/stills/` and `reference/frames/` (git-ignored, never
published) and catalogued in `reference/catalogue.csv`. The contact sheet is
`reference/sheets/sheet_all.jpg`.

## 1. How to read this document

Each statement has an **evidence tier** and a **confidence** rating.

| Tier | Meaning | Can it drive modelling? |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 Film still** | Visible in an official film still or frame | Yes, for what is actually visible |
| **T2 Screenplay** | Written in the 2015 shooting script | It tells us what exists. It does not fix where things are or how they look. The final cut may differ. |
| **T3 Independent / production** | Set photograph, crew interview, prop maker, VFX vendor | Yes, but note that set dressing can change between a photo and the shot |
| **T4 Unknown** | Not established by any source | **No.** This is a placeholder only, and it is marked as such in any blockout |

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Seen directly in at least one sharp image, or quoted from a primary source |
| **Medium** | Seen but small, blurred, partly hidden, or from a secondary report of a primary source |
| **Low** | One weak source, or a fan site with no citation |

### 4.2 T2 — described in the 2015 shooting script (S04)

Scene numbers are the script's own. The final cut may reorder, trim or change them.
Quotes are short and verbatim. They are for reference only; the PDF is not stored in the project.

| Scene | Heading | What the script establishes |
|---|---|---|
| SC39 | EXT. BROWNSTONE STREET – DUSK | Tina hides the men behind a repair truck and looks at "a house across the street". She is "not supposed to have men on the premises". |
| SC40 | INT. GOLDSTEIN RESIDENCE, STAIRWELL – DUSK | They "tiptoe up the stairs". On "the first landing" Mrs Esposito, the landlady, calls from off-screen. |
| SC41 | INT. … SITTING ROOM – DUSK | "Although impoverished, the apartment is enlivened by workaday magic." "An iron is working away on its own in a corner." "A clothes horse revolves clumsily on its wooden legs **in front of the fire**, drying an assortment of underwear." Magazines are scattered: *The Witch's Friend*, *Witch Chat*, *Transfiguration Today*. Queenie stands "in a silk slip, supervising the mending of a dress on a dressmaker's dummy". Newt "starts peeking out of the **windows**" (so the sitting room has windows). Jacob "falls back onto a **sofa**". Newt stands by "the window" and later has his "hand on the doorknob". "TINA and QUEENIE busy themselves in **the kitchen**": ingredients float out of **cupboards**, "carrots and apples chop themselves, pastry rolls itself and pans stir". Tina sends dishes, cutlery and glasses to set "the table". The strudel bakes in mid-air. "TINA lights **candles** on the table." |
| SC44 | SITTING ROOM – HALF AN HOUR LATER – NIGHT | Four at the table: "NEWT and TINA sit opposite each other". Newt "stands behind his chair". Tina moves "towards a **back room**": "you guys can bunk in here". Dialogue: Queenie "spend[s] most days making coffee" at work. |
| SC45 | BEDROOM – NIGHT | "neatly made **twin beds**"; Jacob reads a wizarding book. Tina, in "patterned blue pyjamas", brings "a tray of cocoa", and the mugs stir themselves. A **bedside table** is present. "The toilet's **down the hall to the right**" (outside the apartment). As the door shuts, Jacob glimpses "QUEENIE **in the other room**, wearing a much less demure dressing gown". |
| SC52 | EXT. GOLDSTEIN RESIDENCE – NIGHT | "TINA and QUEENIE lean out of **their bedroom window**", in winter. Neighbours open their windows. |
| SC53 | INT. GOLDSTEIN RESIDENCE – NIGHT | The sisters "burst into the bedroom" where the men should be. Tina "storms off to dress". Queenie: "But we made 'em cocoa…" |

What the script implies about layout (inference, so T4 until checked against images):
- There are at least three spaces: a sitting room with a kitchen and a dining table; a "back room" with twin beds for the men; and the sisters' bedroom, which has a street-facing window.
- The toilet is shared and outside the apartment, down a hall.
- The script never mentions a radio, gramophone or record player.

## 5. Agreements between sources (raise confidence)

- **Fireplace:** the script ("in front of the fire"), R02/R03 (a black ornate surround) and R01 (a black barley-twist surround at the left edge) all agree. The fireplace exists, is black and ornate, and is on the **left wall of the R01 view, nearest the camera**.
- **Dress form and magic mending:** the script SC41, the Pinnock interview and R02–R04 agree.
- **Iron working on its own:** the script SC41, Pinnock ("a magnet underneath the ironing board") and R04 (a pink-covered ironing board) agree.
- **Twin beds and a bedside table:** the script SC45, R01 (two twin beds, nightstand between the windows) and R05 (a bed, a bedside cabinet with a fringed lamp) agree.
- **A window room beyond dark panelled double doors:** R01 and R04 show the same composition: two tall windows, a nightstand and a fringed lamp centred between them. This is a strong, though not conclusive, match between the set photo and a film frame.
- **Dining table and pressed-back chairs:** the script SC41/44 and R01; the R04 foreground chair matches the R01 chair style.

## 7. What can be established without the film

**Enough for a reviewed blockout of one view.** R01 fixes the order of objects along both side walls of
the main room, the position and style of the pocket doors, the kitchen run, the dining group and the
back room's window wall. R02–R05 confirm the fireplace, practical lamps, the bedroom bed, curtains and
lamp, and Queenie's 1926 look.

**Not enough for:** any measurement, the fourth wall, the entrance, the sisters' bedroom, the
sitting-room windows, the ceiling, the film lighting, or anything about the sound.

## 8. References that would most improve accuracy (in order)

1. **Frames from the film itself.** Store purchases, streaming and Blu-ray are all DRM-protected, and
   this project will not decrypt them (see `REFERENCE_CAPTURE_GUIDE.md`). The simplest lawful route is
   **your own screenshots or phone photos of the screen** while you watch a copy you own or rent, for
   scenes SC39–SC45 and SC52–SC53. These go straight into `refs.py import-stills`. A DRM-free file you
   lawfully have (rare) can use the full `extract`, `scan` and `shots` workflow.
2. ***Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them: The Art of the Film*** (Dermot Power, HarperCollins 2016, ISBN 9780008204617). Production paintings, **set blueprints** and concept art; check whether it includes the apartment.
3. ***Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them: The Original Screenplay*** (Little, Brown 2016). The lawful printed text, to confirm S04 wording and spot any changes.
4. **Official trailers and the official "Queenie makes a strudel" clip** on Warner Bros. channels. Watch and screenshot; do not download with rip tools.
5. **Warner Bros. Studio Tour** (London or Hollywood) Fantastic Beasts displays. Visitor photos of apartment props, books and magazines, and reportedly concept prints of the apartment (MuggleNet review, page now 404).
6. **MinaLima** graphic props (magazines, haberdashery) as lawful art prints.
7. **Vanity Fair, Feb 2017** production-design article (S05 original) and the Pottermore 2016 set-strike interview video, for any further wide shots.
